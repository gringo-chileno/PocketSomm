Pocket Somm Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026

Pocket Somm is a wine menu scanner built by an independent developer. The short version: the app collects no data about you.

Everything stays on your phone

Photos you scan are processed entirely on your device using Apple's built-in text recognition. Your ratings, notes, and scan history are stored only on your phone. The wine catalog is bundled inside the app, so lookups happen offline. Nothing you scan, rate, or search is sent to us or anyone else.

No accounts, no tracking

There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Feedback is the one exception

If you choose to use the "Send Feedback" form, the text you write is posted publicly as an issue on the app's GitHub page, along with the app version and your iOS version. Don't include anything personal you wouldn't want public. This only happens when you tap Submit; the app never sends anything on its own.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device, so deleting the app deletes everything. You can also clear ratings and scan history any time in Settings.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
